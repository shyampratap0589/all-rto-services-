Lok Adalat Settlement

Lok Adalat is a settlement forum where eligible pending cases can be disposed of amicably. It is one of the most useful routes for old and accumulated challans — and one of the least understood, which is exactly where experience helps.

  • Eligibility checkNot every challan qualifies — we tell you honestly whether yours does.
  • Application preparationThe paperwork is prepared and filed the way the forum needs it.
  • Hearing follow-upWe track the matter to its conclusion.
  • Closure proofYou get the record showing the case is disposed of.

When Lok Adalat makes sense

  • Multiple pending challansSeveral cases accumulated over the years.
  • Old court-referred casesMatters that have been lying unresolved.
  • Commercial vehicle operatorsFleet owners needing a clean record for fitness or permits.
  • Cases blocking a saleWhen a buyer or bank wants dues cleared before transfer.

Lok Adalat sittings are held on fixed dates set by the authorities, so the timeline follows their calendar — not ours.
