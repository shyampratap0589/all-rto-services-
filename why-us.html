What We Do Not Promise

Most websites in this line promise everything. We would rather set the expectation correctly, because that is what keeps a client for years instead of one job.

  • We are not a government officeWe are a private service assisting you with challan and RTO work.
  • We cannot set the government feeChallan amounts, RTO fees and taxes are decided by the department.
  • We cannot control court datesCourt and Lok Adalat matters follow the forum's own calendar.
  • We will not invent a timelineYou get a realistic estimate, not a promise designed to win the job.
