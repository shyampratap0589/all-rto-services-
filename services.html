NOC & Hypothecation

Two of the most common reasons an RC gets stuck: a vehicle that needs to move to another state, and a loan entry that was never removed after the EMI ended.

  • Inter-state NOCPermission to re-register your vehicle in another state.
  • Intra-state NOCMovement between RTO jurisdictions within the same state.
  • NOC for saleWhere the buyer needs a clear NOC before purchase.
  • Hypothecation removalBank entry deleted from the RC after loan closure.
  • Hypothecation additionFinancer details added for a new or refinanced loan.
  • Re-registration after NOCNew state registration with the tax adjustment handled.

Usually needed for NOC & hypothecation work

  • RC copyOriginal or clear copy of the Registration Certificate.
  • Valid insurancePolicy in force at the time of application.
  • PUC certificateWhere applicable to the vehicle class.
  • ID & address proofAadhaar, voter ID, passport or similar.
  • Bank NOC / Form 35For hypothecation removal, from the financer.
  • Challan clearancePending dues usually must be settled first.

The exact list depends on your state and case — we confirm it for you after a free check.
